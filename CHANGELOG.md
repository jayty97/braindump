# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-09-08

Initial public alpha of BrainDump (desktop name: Dictate Workbench).

- Local recording with pause/resume, microphone selection, level meter, and tray operation.
- Independent speech and writing providers, editable model IDs, and secure optional key storage.
- GPT-Transcribe and GPT-5.6 Luna defaults, with low/medium reasoning.
- Groq, ElevenLabs, Ollama, and compatible custom endpoint options.
- Bounded audio uploads, resumable processing, saved sessions, and editable/exportable prompts.
- Optional NVIDIA Broadcast / RTX Voice virtual microphone recognition.
- MIT licensing, Windows/macOS/Linux setup instructions, and automated tests across those platforms.

Known limits: physical microphone quality and paid provider calls require user-side testing; Linux/macOS audio and tray behavior need manual validation. No native installers are provided yet.
