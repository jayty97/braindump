The MIT license applies to BrainDump's original code. It does not replace licenses belonging to dependencies or service providers.

Dependencies include PySide6/Qt, sounddevice/PortAudio, NumPy, HTTPX, keyring, and their transitive dependencies. Their license files and metadata are distributed by their upstream packages. Qt/PySide6 has its own LGPL/GPL/commercial licensing options. Review the actual installed versions and retain all required notices when distributing a bundled executable.

This release distributes original Python source and a wheel; dependencies are installed separately by pip. NVIDIA Broadcast, RTX Voice, Ollama, and AI model weights are not bundled. Provider names identify integrations and do not imply endorsement.
